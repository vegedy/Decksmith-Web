# Entropy and notation

For a probability vector \(p \in \mathbb{R}^{n}\), uncertainty is:

<Equation id="entropy" label="Shannon entropy" explanation="Zero-probability terms contribute zero.">

\[
H(p) = -\sum_{i=1}^{n} p_i \log_2 p_i
\]

</Equation>

See <EquationRef id="entropy" /> for the definition.
